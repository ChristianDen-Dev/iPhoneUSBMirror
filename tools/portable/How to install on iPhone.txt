Den-Dev Live - install the app on the iPhone
=============================================
The app is not on the App Store. You put it on your iPhone from the laptop
with Sideloadly, using your own free Apple ID.

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  DenDevLive.ipa          the iPhone app
  SideloadlySetup64.exe   Sideloadly (the program that installs the app)

NEEDED ON THE LAPTOP FIRST
  - Apple Devices (or iTunes) so the laptop sees the iPhone.
    "1 - LAPTOP\1 - SETUP (first time).bat" checks and offers it.
  - Sideloadly: double-click SideloadlySetup64.exe (or let SETUP do it).

INSTALL (about 3 minutes)
  1. Plug the iPhone into the laptop with the cable. Unlock it.
     If the iPhone asks "Trust This Computer?" -> Trust -> type the passcode.
  2. Open Sideloadly. Your iPhone shows at the top.
  3. Drag DenDevLive.ipa into the Sideloadly window.
  4. Type YOUR Apple ID (email) in Sideloadly -> Start.
     The password is typed only into Sideloadly, never anywhere else.
  5. Wait for "Done".
  6. On the iPhone: Settings -> General -> VPN & Device Management ->
     tap your Apple ID -> Trust.
  7. First time only (iOS 16 and newer): Settings -> Privacy & Security ->
     Developer Mode -> ON -> Restart -> Turn On.
  8. Open Den-Dev Live. Allow Microphone and Local Network when asked.

EVERY 7 DAYS (free Apple ID)
  The app stops opening after 7 days. Do steps 1-5 again (about 2 minutes).
  Your settings and stream key stay.

PROBLEMS
  - Sideloadly does not see the iPhone: install Apple Devices, unplug/replug,
    unlock, tap Trust. Try another cable or USB port.
  - "Untrusted Developer": do step 6.
  - App closes at once: do step 7 (Developer Mode).
